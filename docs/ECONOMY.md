# Fictional economy

No real money, deposits, withdrawals or payment processor exists.

New guests receive69 persistent credits once, recorded in the ledger. A separate69-credit life allocation resets only on a validated new life and is never deposited in the tradable wallet. Reconnect, map switching and custody release do not mint persistent money. Life-equipment spending is not yet comprehensive.

Server-observed events award score/credits using persistent location+event IDs. Balances/amounts are safe integers with no overdraft. Transactions and durable request receipts prevent duplicate charges and settlements, including restart/retry. Reusing an ID with different payload fails.

Spectators can buy/sell four fictional instruments; no negative holdings, shorts, loans or real-world securities. Quantities are bounded. Quotes reflect observed tension/damage/violence; orders do not directly move prices. Fee:2%, minimum1 credit.

Prediction contracts last60/120 simulation seconds: selected person stays free, Book capture, Book defense or high unrest. Stakes are integers capped50 and20% of balance; maximum5 open contracts. Fixed2x odds at entry. Server remembers intervening detention/death and capture rather than trusting the latest screen. Unresolved disappearing targets are refunded. This is a simple game mechanic, not a calibrated exchange.

Supply funding creates an actual route-following truck with finite crates. Essentials cost 20 credits and deliver food/water and first aid. Protester equipment costs 60 (stick, bat, shield, stones); police equipment costs 70 (shields and gas). A protester arms delivery costs 500 and contains one human-only fictional sidearm with three rounds. Prices come from the server catalog, not client-submitted amounts. Capacity is bounded. Open positions/predictions block funding; funding blocks speculation for 60 simulation seconds. These single-account controls do not solve alternate-account collusion. Public competitive economics requires stronger identity and abuse controls.

Constitution Book capture and defense award the leading eligible human 15 or10 credits, respectively. Active human contributors receive3 credits each plus a5-credit team reward; NPCs cannot contribute capture progress or receive player payouts. Event IDs make these settlements idempotent across restart.

Persistent markets share one 1.5-second server quote cadence across both cities. Prices ease toward values derived from observed tension, damage and violence rather than alternating per-city and global updates. UI candlesticks aggregate actual price samples; they are not forecasts. Prediction odds remain uncalibrated and credits have no real-money value.
